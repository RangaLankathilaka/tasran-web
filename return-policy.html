1. Custom Software Development & Project Services

1.1 Non-Refundable Deliverables

Tasran Digital Solutions provides bespoke, custom software development services that are tailored specifically to your unique business requirements, specifications, and project scope. Because these services involve significant custom development effort, specialized expertise, and deliverables created exclusively for your project:

Custom development services, source code, designs, and deliverables are NON-REFUNDABLE once completed and delivered to the client.

  • Business application solutions and integrations
  • Source code, documentation, and deliverables
  • Architecture design and system consultation
  • Completed project phases and milestones

1.2 Project Payment Terms

We typically structure projects using milestone-based payments:

  • Initial Payment: Due at project commencement or contract signing
  • Milestone Payments: Due upon completion and approval of specific project phases
  • Final Payment: Due upon final delivery and project acceptance
Important: Payments for completed and approved milestones are non-refundable. Each milestone payment is considered final upon delivery and client sign-off.

1.3 Client Satisfaction & Revisions

We are committed to your satisfaction. Project estimates typically include a reasonable number of revision rounds (usually 2-3 rounds per milestone). If you are not satisfied with deliverables before milestone sign-off:

  • We will work with you to make reasonable revisions and improvements
  • Revisions must be requested before formal milestone acceptance
  • Unlimited revisions may incur additional development fees
  • Once you formally approve and sign off on a milestone, we cannot provide refunds

2. Service Cancellation Policies

2.1 Project Cancellation by Client

If you decide to cancel a custom development project before completion, you have the right to do so with written notice to tasran.digi@gmail.com.

2.2 Cancellation Terms & Responsibilities

  • Payment Obligation: You are responsible for paying all work completed up to the date of cancellation notice
  • Partial Refunds: If a project is cancelled mid-way, you will owe payment only for completed milestones and work in progress at the time of cancellation
  • Intellectual Property: Upon full payment, all completed work and source code becomes your property
  • Deliverables: We will provide all completed deliverables, documentation, and source code upon payment settlement
Example: If a project has 5 milestones and you cancel after 3 are completed, you pay for the 3 completed milestones plus any reasonable costs for partially completed work, and we deliver all completed deliverables.

2.3 Cancellation Request Process

  1. Send written cancellation notice to tasran.digi@gmail.com
  2. Include project name, reference number, and effective cancellation date
  3. We will calculate final amount due for work completed
  4. Settlement of outstanding balances is required before final deliverable transfer
  5. Processing typically takes 5-7 business days
